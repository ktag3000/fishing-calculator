Bass Fishing Conditions

Auto-filled from your location's weather, spanning the last 10 days and next 10 days.

How this works

Tap "Use my location" to auto-fill everything from your GPS position, "Enter manually" to type a latitude/longitude, or "ZIP code or city/state" to look up a US ZIP or a place name. Once a location loads, you can name and save it. Saved spots appear as chips you can tap to reload anytime, and the app remembers the last location you loaded so it isn't blank the next time you open it.

The strip shows the last 10 days and the next 10, each labeled with its total score. Tap any day to load its weather into the inputs below. Today is outlined; the day you've selected is highlighted in orange.

Once a location is loaded, two tabs appear above the day's details. "Score" shows the fishing score and everything that feeds it. "Weather" shows the plain hourly forecast for whichever day is selected, every factor the score uses: temperature, barometric pressure, cloud cover, rain, and wind hour by hour, plus moon phase and water temp for the day as a whole. It also includes a quick reference table for reading pressure on its own.

Tap the ⓘ next to "Select a day" to see which upcoming days look most promising: falling pressure, an approaching front, overcast skies, calm or gusty wind, and moon phase are all called out when they're notable.

Tap the 📈 icon to expand a line graph of pressure (in inHg) across all 21 days. It opens centered on today, marked with a highlighted dot and a dashed guide line, so you can see at a glance whether pressure has been falling, rising, or holding steady around now.

Shows first light, sunrise, sunset, and last light for the selected day. Sunrise and sunset come from real weather data; first and last light are estimated as civil twilight using your latitude and the date. Below that, "Best window" compares the dawn and dusk light windows for the selected day using cloud cover and wind, and tells you which one looks more favorable.

Pressure trend, wind, cloud cover, precipitation, and moon phase are auto-filled from real weather data for whichever day you pick, but every field stays editable if you want to try different conditions.

Water temperature is estimated from a 5-day trailing air-temp average (there's no free public API for actual lake temperature). Adjust it if you have real data. Bass season is inferred from that estimate, or you can override it manually with the dropdown.

The big number is a 0-10 score combining all six weighted factors plus a bonus or penalty for the current spawn phase. Below it, a plain-language summary and a grid list out every metric (pressure, wind, cloud cover, precipitation, moon phase, water temp) for the day. The bars further down show how each individual factor scored.

TrendTypical inHgBass activity

General guideline, not a hard rule: this table reflects the pressure level itself. The score's pressure factor works a bit differently: a rapidly falling barometer is the one time pressure "on the move" is a strong trigger; otherwise, a shifting barometer usually means fish are still acclimating and biting less, and the best bite tends to show up 2-3 days after pressure has stabilized.
